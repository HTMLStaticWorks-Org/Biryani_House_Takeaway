Every handi sealed at Biryani House honors centuries-old royal Nizami tenets—where recipes remain sacred, embers burn slow, and genuine dum cooking is never compromised.

PILLAR I • HERITAGE ARCHIVES

Time-Honored Nizami Recipes

PILLAR II • NO SHORTCUTS

Pristine Daily Procurement

100% fresh, tender halal meats marinated 12 hours prior with whole spices. Pure Kashmiri saffron, house-churned ghee, and 2-year aged Extra Long Grain Basmati rice.

PILLAR III • LIVE EMBERS

Authentic Purdah Dum Cooking

Heavy copper and clay handis sealed airtight with wheat dough (Purdah). Slow-simmered over live charcoal embers so every grain absorbs rich meat juices and delicate floral aroma.

100% Slow Coal Simmered

PILLAR IV • ROYAL HOSPITALITY

Regal Dastarkhwan Portions
