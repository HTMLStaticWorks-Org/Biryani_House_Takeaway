ROOTED IN TRADITION. MADE FOR TODAY.

Biryani House began with an uncompromising conviction: authentic dum cooking should never be compromised for haste or industrial shortcuts.

Our culinary philosophy treats each preparation as a multi-stage ceremony: marinating fresh prime cuts overnight in velvety curd, blooming hand-crushed whole cloves and cardamom in pure ghee, and slow-cooking under a dough seal until the grains absorb the depth of every spice.

THE ART OF DUM COOKING

A step-by-step glimpse into the rigorous traditional culinary process that defines our signature taste.

Phase 01 • Provenance 01
Step 01 of 05 • Foundation

Select Ingredients

Two-Year Aged Extra-Long Basmati & Prime Farm Meats

Two-year aged basmati rice grains and tender farm meats inspected for grade. Only elongated grains with minimal moisture and sublime natural aroma are selected for royal dum authenticity.

2-Year Aged Basmati Grade-A Farm Cuts Grain Sorting Inspection
“The grain must stand separate and proud, like pearls in a royal crown.”
Stage 01 / 05
Phase 02 • Alchemy 02
Step 02 of 05 • Spice Craft

Prepare Spices

Whole Cardamom, Shahi Jeera & Stone-Ground Masalas

Whole cardamom pods, black cloves, shahi jeera, star anise, and pure saffron stone-ground fresh every dawn. Ground cold at room temperature to preserve delicate essential oils.

Whole Green Cardamom Pure Kashmiri Saffron Stone-Ground Fresh
“Spices awaken in warm ghee, releasing royal fragrance before meeting the grain.”
Stage 02 / 05
Phase 03 • Architecture 03
Step 03 of 05 • Layering Art

Layer Rice & Masala

70% Parboiled Rice, Golden Barista & Desi Ghee

Precise alternating tiers of 70% parboiled rice, seasoned meat, caramelized golden onions (barista), garden-fresh mint, and pure warm desi ghee to lock in flavor gradients.

Golden Barista Onions Pure Desi Ghee Fresh Mint & Kewra
“Every tier traps steam at a different temperature gradient for tender perfection.”
Stage 03 / 05
Phase 04 • Purdah (Seal) 04
Step 04 of 05 • Pressure Seal

Seal the Handi

Airtight Wheat Flour Dough Barrier on Heavy Copper

Airtight wheat flour dough barrier firmly applied along the copper handi rim to prevent steam from escaping. It transforms the vessel into a self-pressurized aromatic oven.

Traditional Atta Purdah Zero Steam Leakage Heavy Copper Handi
“Not a single drop of fragrant moisture is allowed to escape into the open air.”
Stage 04 / 05
Phase 05 • Dum Pukht 05
Step 05 of 05 • Dum Simmer

Slow Cook to Perfection

Gentle Charcoal Embers & Circulatory Steam Basting

Gentle indirect heat allows trapped moisture to baste the rice grains into delicate perfection. Internal circulatory vapor tenderizes the meat until it melts like butter.

45-Min Charcoal Dum Self-Basting Vapor Pearl-Separated Rice
“When the seal is broken at your table, 400 years of royal heritage bursts forth.”
