Our Heritage & Passion

OUR STORY IS COOKED INTO EVERY LAYER.

At Biryani House & Takeaway, cooking biryani is not merely a recipe — it is a time-honored discipline of balance, patient heat, and deep respect for spice origins.

The Heart of Hospitality

TRADITIONAL DUM AT HEART.

Founded with the simple ambition to celebrate the depth and honesty of traditional Hyderabadi and South Indian dum cooking.

Our story revolves around the communal spirit of the Indian dining table: friends, families and colleagues gathered around an aromatic degh, steam wafting upward as the dough seal is broken with a silver spoon.

Authentic kitchen recipes, family-inspired spice blends, and welcoming hospitality tailored for everyday takeaway and festive family celebrations.

OUR COOKING PHILOSOPHY

Six uncompromised standards guiding our master ustads from morning prep to final tableside serving.

01
Pillar I • Provenance

Fresh Ingredients

Strict farm-to-kitchen sourcing of tender meats, farm eggs, crisp seasonal vegetables, and pure golden desi ghee. Zero frozen substitutes.

Farm-to-Kitchen Traceability
02
Pillar II • Alchemy

Carefully Selected Spices

Directly sourced whole cardamom, cinnamon quills, mace flowers, cloves, and pure Kashmiri saffron hand-crushed in-house at room temperature.

Cold In-House Stone Crushing
03
Pillar III • Selection

Quality Aged Rice

Two-year matured extra-long grain basmati cooked to precise 70% tenderness before layering to absorb deep meat broths and saffron steam.

24-Month Grain Maturation
04
Pillar IV • Purdah Heat

Traditional Dum Method

Heavy copper handis sealed with kneaded wheat dough, trapping the volatile aromatics and infusing moisture over slow charcoal heat.

Zero Steam Pressure Loss
05
Pillar V • Discipline

Consistent Preparation

Standardized slow-cook timings and precise spice-ratio balances ensuring the exact same exquisite royal taste every single visit.

100% Recipe Consistency
06
Pillar VI • Integrity

Fresh Serving Guarantee

Every handi is broken fresh upon order, ensuring hot fragrant rice and succulent meats never sit reheated or microwave-treated.

Broken Fresh Upon Order
