Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Browse the menu, click "Add to Order", review your selections in the My Cart view, and click "Proceed to Order".

Can I cancel or modify an order?

Since our handis are sealed under dum cooking immediately upon confirmation, please contact our kitchen hotline directly within 5 minutes of placing an order.

How can I track my delivery?

Navigate to the Track Delivery tab to observe the simulated preparation timeline: Placed, Confirmed, Preparing under Dum, Out for Delivery, and Delivered.

Can I place bulk or catering orders here?

Yes, visit the Party Orders page on our website to submit a dedicated bulk catering inquiry with custom degh portion requirements.
